VisualTalk Pro

The same lightweight meetings, plus a confidentiality guarantee for the things you type and send.

End-to-end encrypted chat and file sharing. Messages and files are encrypted on your device and decrypted only on the other participants' devices — your LiveKit server, whoever operates it, sees ciphertext. Keys are agreed with elliptic-curve cryptography (ECDH P-256) and every message gets its own AES-256-GCM key.

Loud safety rails. Participant keys are pinned on first use: if a key ever changes unexpectedly, Pro stops and warns you instead of silently decrypting. Replayed or mis-sendered messages are refused.

Organizer streaming. Broadcast your meeting to an RTMP destination of your choice (your own CDN or streaming service), from your own LiveKit server. The broadcast layout deliberately excludes chat and files.

Commercial licensing and paid support. Pro carries its own EULA for professional use, with paid support agreements available.

What Pro is not

  • Not a replacement for the Community Edition. CE stays free, open-source and fully functional for meetings on your own server — video, audio, screen share, chat, files, scheduling, recording. Pro is CE plus the confidentiality layer, sold as a separate app with its own icon.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted video or audio. In both editions, meeting media travels through your LiveKit server (over TLS to it). What Pro encrypts end-to-end is chat and file content — we say so plainly because overclaiming here would be a security lie.
  • Not an account, a cloud or a subscription trap. No accounts and no analytics in either edition; Pro works with the same self-hosted server bundle described in the How-to.
