VisualTalk

Private video meetings on your own LiveKit server.

Video meetings that don’t drain your phone.

When you hang up, VisualTalk truly goes to sleep: nothing keeps running in the background, so between calls it costs your battery nothing at all.

The whole app is a small download — a few megabytes, about two or three photos — and the only things it keeps on your device are the ones you save yourself. During a call it borrows only what the call needs, and gives it all back when you leave.

Why it’s so light on your battery, in plain words →

VisualTalk is a lightweight video-conferencing client: bring your own server, keep your recordings on your device, and chat or share files inside the meeting. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no conferencing infrastructure operated by us.
