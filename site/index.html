VisualTalk

Private video meetings on your own LiveKit server.

VisualTalk is a lightweight video-conferencing client: bring your own server, keep your recordings on your device, and chat or share files inside the meeting. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no conferencing infrastructure operated by us.
