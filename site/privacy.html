VisualTalk — Privacy Policy

VISUALTALK — PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 13, 2026 Applies to: VisualTalk Community Edition and VisualTalk Pro Edition (macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android)

  1. THE SHORT VERSION

VisualTalk does not collect your personal data.

  • No user account, no sign-in, no registration.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers of any kind are present in the Software.
  • No server operated by Miradata sits in the path of your meetings.
  • Your profile, preferences, notes, meeting list and recordings are stored on your own device and are never transmitted to us.

VisualTalk is bring-your-own-server. You tell the app which LiveKit server to use, and your audio, video, chat and files travel directly between your device and that server. We never see them and we cannot access them.

  1. BRING-YOUR-OWN-SERVER ARCHITECTURE
  1. You configure your own LiveKit server in Settings → LiveKit by entering a server URL, an API key and an API secret.
  2. The Software ships with no default server and no embedded credentials. If you have not configured a server, meetings cannot start. The app will not silently fall back to any server operated by the Licensor.
  3. Connection tokens (JWT) are generated on your own device using the credentials you entered, and are valid for 24 hours. They are not issued by us and we never see them.
  4. The operator of the server you select — which may be you, your employer, or a commercial provider such as LiveKit Cloud — is responsible for the data that server processes, for its retention policy, and for any server-side recording. Read that operator’s privacy policy.
  5. When you join a meeting hosted by someone else, the host’s organisation controls that server and its recording settings.

We deliberately make it impossible for the Licensor to be inserted into your media path by default.

  1. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

All of the following stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Profile: display name, user identity, email address (only if you choose to enter one), and your personal meeting ID.
  • Preferences: start-with-video, mute-on-join, use-personal-meeting- ID, always-show-preview, and preferred microphone, camera and speaker identifiers.
  • Notes you write in the Notes tab.
  • Scheduled meetings and meeting history: title, room identifier, start time, duration and timestamps.
  • Your LiveKit server URL, API key and API secret.
  • Optional AI assistant configuration, including its API key (see Section 6).
  • Local recordings you make, saved in the app’s recordings folder.
  • Calendar (.ics) files you generate, which may contain the email addresses of the attendees you invited.

These files live in your operating system’s per-user application configuration directory for VisualTalk.

IMPORTANT — PLAINTEXT STORAGE. These values, including your server API secret and any AI API key, are stored as plaintext configuration files and are not held in the operating system keychain. Anyone with access to your device or user account can read them. Protect your device with full-disk encryption and a strong login password. We cannot retrieve, rotate or reset these credentials for you, because we never receive them.

If you schedule a meeting and add attendees, their email addresses are written into the generated .ics file on your device and are included in the invitation you choose to send. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to share those addresses.

DELETION. This data persists until you remove it. You can delete individual items in the app (notes, scheduled meetings, recordings), reset your profile in Settings, or delete everything by removing the app’s configuration directory or uninstalling the Software.

  1. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
  • Camera and microphone: requested through your operating system’s permission prompt and used only while you are in a meeting or in the pre-join preview. They are never accessed in the background.
  • Screen sharing: captured only after you explicitly start sharing, and stops when you end it.
  • Clipboard: written to only when you copy a meeting ID or invitation.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your operating system’s privacy settings. The Software will continue to run with the affected feature disabled.

  1. OPTIONAL AI ASSISTANT (DISABLED BY DEFAULT)

The AI chat assistant is turned off unless you enable it in Settings → AI Assistant.

  • When enabled, it sends your prompt together with the last 20 chat turns of the meeting as context to the endpoint you configure.
  • The default configuration points at a server running on your own machine (http://localhost:11434/v1, for example Ollama or LM Studio). With a local endpoint your conversation never leaves your device.
  • If you point the assistant at a cloud provider such as OpenAI or OpenRouter, that provider receives your prompt and chat history and processes it under its own privacy policy. Miradata is not involved in that exchange, has no relationship with that provider, and receives nothing.
  • Because this is a meaningful disclosure, the Software shows a one-time consent prompt when the configured endpoint is not on localhost.
  • Your AI API key is stored locally on your device (see Section 4).
  1. ENCRYPTION

Pro Edition — End-to-End Encryption. E2EE covers chat messages and file transfers: they are encrypted on your device before transmission and can only be decrypted by meeting participants who have completed the key exchange. The server operator cannot read that content. The cryptographic stack is ECDH P-256 for key exchange, HKDF-SHA-256 for key derivation, and AES-256-GCM for symmetric encryption.

IMPORTANT — AUDIO AND VIDEO ARE NOT END-TO-END ENCRYPTED. In both editions, audio and video streams are protected in transit by the LiveKit server’s transport security (TLS and DTLS-SRTP) but are processable by the operator of the server you chose. If you require media that the server operator cannot access, VisualTalk is not the right tool today.

Community Edition — no E2EE. Chat messages are not end-to-end encrypted and are processable by the operator of the server you chose.

Miradata receives no personal data from the Software, and therefore makes no international transfer of your data.

The LiveKit server and any AI endpoint you configure may be hosted in any country. Any transfer that results is between you and that provider, and is governed by that provider’s terms. If you use VisualTalk for work, your organisation should verify that the server it selects offers appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V.

  1. SECURITY
  • No credentials are embedded in the distributed application. A signing secret compiled into a shipped binary is recoverable by anyone, so the Software requires you to supply your own and keeps them on your device.
  • Connections to your LiveKit server use encrypted transport (wss:// over TLS).
  • Connection tokens are generated locally and expire after 24 hours.
  • Recording files are confined to the app’s own recordings directory.

The security of your stored credentials ultimately depends on the security of your device — see the plaintext-storage warning in Section 4.
