# Market

A deploy-anywhere, LiveKit-compatible conferencing layer for organizations that need control over where their real-time communications run.

## 1. The Sovereign Cloud Imperative (France/EU)

The French and EU markets are driven by a regulatory push for digital sovereignty. US-cloud SaaS tools (Teams, Zoom, Meet) are increasingly disqualified for critical infrastructure due to:
- **SecNumCloud (ANSSI):** French standard for sovereign cloud security.
- **GDPR & Cloud Act:** Conflict of laws regarding data access.
- **Gaia-X:** European federated data infrastructure initiative.

## 2. Target Market: French OIVs & Defense

**OIVs (Operators of Vital Importance)** and **Defense** entities are legally mandated to secure their critical infrastructure. They cannot use foreign-controlled SaaS for sensitive communications.

- **Beachhead:** French OIVs, Defense, and Sovereign Cloud providers seeking **SecNumCloud** qualification.
- **Expansion:** Broader EU government and regulated sectors (healthcare, energy, finance) under NIS2 and GDPR.

## 3. Competitive Landscape

- **US Titans (Teams/Zoom/Meet):** Disqualified by infrastructure control and data flow regulations.
- **Consumer E2EE (WhatsApp/Signal):** Disqualified by lack of admin control and enterprise compliance.
- **Jitsi:** Open-source, but lacks the integrated sovereign product and compliance focus.

## 4. Strategic Conclusion

VisualTalk wins by controlling the four pillars of sovereignty: **Infrastructure, Keys, Operations, and Data Flows.** We cede the general market and dominate the legally mandated sovereign niche in France first.
